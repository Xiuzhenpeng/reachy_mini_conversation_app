What is inside

All-in-one conversational layer for your robot

The app blends self-hosted speech, vision, and motion so Reachy Mini feels present.

ASR

Natural voice chat

Talk freely and get replies from your own OpenAI-compatible ASR, LLM, and TTS stack.

CAM

Vision-aware tools

Ask the camera tool to capture what is in front, track a face, or keep attention on whoever is speaking.

MOVE

Expressive motion

Queue dances and recorded emotions while Reachy listens and talks.

CFG

Profiles on demand

Switch conversation styles through profiles and decide which tools each persona can use.

Pipeline

Microphone to speech output

Local audio frames are segmented with VAD, sent to your ASR endpoint, passed through chat completions with optional tool calls, and rendered back to robot speech through your TTS endpoint.
